# Marketing Hub

## Goal

Market the funeral home's own services separately from memorials. Publish digital brochures, promotions, branch details, and contact information on a public services page, then track how families engage with them.

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes  
**Requirements:** An authorised funeral home account.

## Overview

The Marketing Hub gives every funeral home its own public page at `/fh/{slug}`. Brochures each get their own page at `/services/{slug}` and are linked from memorials through the "Services from {Funeral Home}" section.

The Marketing Hub sections in the sidebar are:

- Marketing Dashboard
- Digital Brochures
- Categories
- Promotions
- Branches
- Contact Information
- Enquiries
- Analytics

## Marketing Dashboard

1. Sign in and open **Marketing** in the sidebar.
2. Review the summary cards:
   - Published brochures and total views.
   - Active promotions.
   - Total QR scans, PDF downloads, and phone clicks.
   - Recent enquiries.

## Contact Information

1. Open **Marketing > Contact Information**.
2. Enter the business phone, WhatsApp number, email, and physical address.
3. Add social links (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok) and the website URL.
4. Upload a logo and a cover image for the services page.
5. Toggle which modules appear on the public page (brochures, promotions, branches, contact).
6. Save. The changes appear on the public services page immediately.

> **Tip:** The contact details can differ from the login profile branding — use the public-facing number families should call.

## Brochure Categories

1. Open **Marketing > Categories**.
2. Click **Add Category** and enter a name (for example "Funeral Packages" or "Burials").
3. Categories organise brochures and appear as filters on the public services page.

> **Note:** A category with brochures assigned cannot be deleted until the brochures are moved or removed.

## Digital Brochures

1. Open **Marketing > Digital Brochures** and click **New Brochure**.
2. Fill in the basics:
   - **Title** and **Category**
   - **Short URL slug** (auto-generated from the title)
3. Write the **description** families will read.
4. Upload a **cover image** and optionally a **PDF** version of the brochure.
5. Add **gallery photos** to showcase the service.
6. Set the **call to action**: button text, link, WhatsApp number, phone number, or email.
7. Add **SEO title and description** for search engines.
8. Set **display order** and mark the brochure as **published** and **featured** when ready.
9. Save. Use the **QR code** button to download a scannable code for print.

## Promotions

1. Open **Marketing > Promotions** and click **New Promotion**.
2. Enter the title, optional banner image, and description.
3. Choose the **start and end dates**.
4. Optionally set a button text and link.
5. Publish it. Active promotions appear on the public services page between their dates.

## Branches

1. Open **Marketing > Branches**.
2. Add each branch with phone, email, address, city, province, and country.
3. Enter the **manager** and **business hours**.
4. Add **GPS coordinates** and a **Google Maps link** for directions.
5. Branches appear on the public services page with a map-ready card for each.

## Enquiries

1. Open **Marketing > Enquiries**.
2. Review enquiries submitted from public brochure pages.
3. Open an enquiry to read the full message and the brochure it came from.
4. Delete enquiries once they have been handled.

## Marketing Analytics

1. Open **Marketing > Analytics**.
2. Review total views, unique visitors, QR scans, PDF downloads, phone/WhatsApp clicks, and CTA clicks.
3. View the views-by-day chart and the brochure ranking.
4. Watch the **conversion rate** (enquiries compared to views) to see which brochures turn into leads.

## Public Services Page

- The funeral home page is at `https://memoryconnect.co.za/fh/{slug}`.
- Each brochure is at `https://memoryconnect.co.za/services/{slug}`.
- Published memorials show a "Services from {Funeral Home}" section with buttons to explore packages, view promotions, and contact the team.

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting to mark a brochure as **published** (it stays hidden otherwise).
- Publishing a promotion without a title or dates.
- Entering a contact number on the profile but a different one on the services page.
- Expecting QR scans to count before the QR code is scanned on a device.
