# Vehicles & Assets

## Goal

Manage the physical resources of the funeral home: service vehicles and reusable equipment. Track registrations, insurance, maintenance, movements, and damage so nothing is missed on funeral day.

**Estimated time:** 45 minutes  
**Requirements:** An authorised funeral home account.

## Overview

The **Operations** group in the sidebar contains:

- **Vehicles** - hearses, family cars, minibuses, trucks, and utility vehicles.
- **Assets** - reusable equipment such as tents, chairs, tables, stretchers, sound systems, and generators.
- **Funeral Resources** - the planning screen that reserves vehicles and assets for each memorial (see [Funeral Resources](15-funeral-resources.md)).

## Vehicle Dashboard

1. Open **Vehicles** in the sidebar.
2. Review the summary cards:
   - Total vehicles.
   - Available, booked, and in maintenance.
   - Inactive vehicles.
   - Licenses expiring soon (within 30 days).
3. Use the search box and filters for vehicle type and status.

## Adding a Vehicle

1. Click **Add Vehicle**.
2. Enter the **name** and **registration number**.
3. Choose the **vehicle type**: Hearse, Family Car, Minibus, Truck, or Utility.
4. Add the **manufacturer, model, year, VIN, colour, capacity**, and **current mileage**.
5. Choose the **fuel type** and **transmission**.
6. Set the **status**: Available, Booked, Maintenance, or Inactive.
7. Assign a **driver** if one is dedicated to the vehicle.
8. Add **insurance company and policy number**, plus the **expiry dates** for insurance, roadworthy, and license disk.
9. Set the **next service date**.
10. Add notes and save.

## Vehicle Details & Records

Select a vehicle to open the detail panel with tabs:

- **Photos** - upload photos of the vehicle and remove outdated ones.
- **Service History** - record each service: date, mileage, cost, provider, and next service date.
- **Fuel Log** - track fuel purchases with amount, cost, mileage, and station.
- **Maintenance** - log maintenance jobs with type, date, cost, provider, and status (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed).
- **Documents** - upload insurance certificates, roadworthy certificates, and license disks.

> **Warning:** Expiry dates do not trigger external reminders. Use the dashboard "expiring soon" count and the next-service fields to plan renewals.

## Asset Dashboard

1. Open **Assets** in the sidebar.
2. Review the summary cards: total assets, counts by status, and warranties expiring soon.
3. Filter by category, status, or condition.

## Adding an Asset

1. Click **Add Asset**.
2. Enter the **name** and choose the **category**: Tent, Chair, Table, Stretcher, Lowering Device, Sound System, Generator, Coffin Trolley, Gazebo, Flowers, Flags, Display Screen, Projector, Lighting, or Office Equipment.
3. Add the **serial number** if available.
4. Record the **purchase date and price**, **supplier**, and **warranty expiry**.
5. Set the **branch** and **current location**.
6. Choose the **condition**: Excellent, Good, Needs Repair, or Broken.
7. Set the **status**: Available, Reserved, In Use, Maintenance, or Retired.
8. Assign an **employee** responsible for the asset and add notes.

## Asset Details & Records

Select an asset to open the detail panel with tabs:

- **Photos** - upload photos of the asset.
- **Documents** - upload receipts, manuals, and warranty certificates.
- **Movement History** - record every move between branches or locations, with who moved it and when.
- **Damage Reports** - log damage with a description, severity (Minor to Critical), status, and repair cost.
- **QR Code** - generate a scannable QR code for the asset. Print it and attach it to the physical asset for check-in/check-out.

## Using Asset QR Codes

1. Open the asset and go to the **QR Code** tab.
2. Click to generate the code (one is created automatically the first time).
3. Print and attach the code to the asset.
4. Scan the code to open the asset record so staff can check it in and out.

## Common Mistakes

- Entering the current mileage as a total instead of updating it after each trip.
- Forgetting to move an asset's status to Maintenance while it is being repaired.
- Not recording a movement when equipment moves to another branch.
- Expecting the system to auto-detect damage — damage must be reported manually.
