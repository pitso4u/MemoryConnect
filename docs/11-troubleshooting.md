# Troubleshooting

Use this guide before contacting support. Record the user's email, funeral home, memorial name, time of the problem, and any payment reference. Never record a password.

## Cannot Log In

1. Confirm the correct email address.
2. Check Caps Lock.
3. Try once more carefully.
4. Ask an administrator for a password reset link.
5. If the funeral home is suspended, contact platform support.

> **Warning:** Repeated attempts may appear in failed-login support records.

## Forgot Password

1. Contact the funeral home administrator or platform owner.
2. Request a reset link.
3. Open the newest link.
4. Choose a new private password.

No administrator can read the old password.

## Invitation Expired

1. Contact the funeral home.
2. Ask the administrator to open the memorial's invitation list.
3. Select **Resend Invite**.
4. Open only the newest link.

## Invitation Opens the Wrong Memorial

Do not accept it. Contact the funeral home and provide the invitee name and deceased name shown.

## Payment Failed or Is Pending

1. Do not pay again immediately.
2. Check whether money was deducted.
3. Record the Paystack reference.
4. Review the Payments page.
5. Contact support if the status does not update.

## QR Code Does Not Open

1. Confirm the memorial is published.
2. Scan the QR code from another phone.
3. Confirm the QR code was not cropped or stretched.
4. Confirm it does not point to `localhost`.
5. Download a fresh QR code and test again.

## Directions Are Incorrect

1. Open the memorial's Locations section.
2. Select **Open in Google Maps**.
3. Confirm latitude and longitude.
4. Correct the pin before the memorial locks.
5. Do not use **Use my current location** unless standing at the venue.

## Photos Will Not Upload

1. Check internet access.
2. Try a smaller JPG or PNG image.
3. Upload fewer files at one time.
4. Confirm the memorial is not locked.
5. Refresh once and check whether the photo already uploaded.

## Cannot Publish

1. Confirm you are a Funeral Home Admin, not a family user.
2. Confirm the memorial is a valid draft or payment-pending memorial.
3. Check Paystack configuration and payment status.
4. Confirm required memorial information is present.
5. Record the error and payment reference for support.

## Cannot Edit the Programme

The memorial may be locked 72 hours after publication. Locked programmes cannot be edited by funeral home or family users.

## Memorial Has Expired

Public viewing ends 30 days after publication. Guests see a respectful unavailable message. Contact platform support only if the displayed date appears incorrect.

## Page Shows 403

Your account does not have permission for that area.

- Family users cannot access billing, publishing, settings, staff, or platform pages.
- Funeral home users cannot access the platform owner dashboard.
- Only SUPER_ADMIN can use `/admin-owner`.

## Browser or Page Problem

1. Save any text you have typed elsewhere.
2. Refresh the page once.
3. Sign out and sign in again.
4. Try a current Chrome or Edge browser.
5. Check the internet connection.

## Information to Send Support

- Your name and role.
- Funeral home name.
- Account email.
- Memorial name.
- Approximate time of the problem.
- Payment reference, if relevant.
- Screenshot of the error without passwords or card information.
