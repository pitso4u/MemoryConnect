# Frequently Asked Questions

## How much does Memory Connect cost?

Drafts are free. Publishing a memorial costs **R299.99 once per funeral memorial**.

## How long is a memorial online?

Public viewing lasts 30 days after successful publication.

## Can I edit after publishing?

Authorised funeral home users may make permitted edits during the first 72 hours. After that, the memorial is locked.

## What happens after 30 days?

Public viewing ends. The memorial is scheduled for full deletion after the 90-day retention period.

## Can I delete a memorial?

Funeral home administrators may delete eligible drafts or unlocked memorials. SUPER_ADMIN may permanently delete abusive or authorised test memorials with confirmation and an audit record. Family users cannot delete memorials.

## How do I invite family?

Open the memorial, create a Family Manager or Family Contributor invitation, then use Copy Link, WhatsApp, or email to share it.

## Can family members publish?

No. Family users cannot publish, pay, delete, access billing, manage staff, or open funeral home settings.

## What is the difference between Family Manager and Family Contributor?

A Family Manager can manage more assigned memorial content before it locks. A Family Contributor uploads or suggests content with more limited control.

## Can I change funeral home branding?

Yes. Funeral Home Admin users can update the approved profile, logo URLs, colours, typography, contact details, social links, and visual templates.

## Can an administrator see my password?

No. Passwords and password hashes are never displayed. An administrator can only generate a secure reset link.

## Why did my invitation stop working?

It may have expired or been replaced by a newer invitation. Ask the funeral home to resend it.

## Why is the programme locked?

Editing locks 72 hours after publication to protect content that has already been shared.

## Does refreshing increase the view count?

Not repeatedly. The same visitor is counted at most once per memorial per day.

## Do preview visits count as public views?

No. Admin and family previews are excluded.

## How do guests find directions?

Guests choose a saved venue on the public memorial and open it in Google Maps.

## Who pays for publishing?

The funeral home completes the Paystack publishing payment. Family accounts do not receive billing access.

## What should I do if payment is pending?

Wait briefly, check whether money was deducted, record the Paystack reference, and review the Payments page before retrying.

## Can a funeral home see another funeral home's memorials?

No. Funeral home users are limited to their own organisation. Family users see only assigned memorials.

## Who can access the owner dashboard?

Only a user with the SUPER_ADMIN role.

## Are platform owner actions recorded?

Sensitive actions and funeral-home detail viewing are recorded in platform audit logs.

## Can I reuse a QR code for another memorial?

No. Each QR code points to one memorial. Download and test the correct QR code for every service.
